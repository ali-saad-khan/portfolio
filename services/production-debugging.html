Laravel & PHP Production Debugging

When something is broken in production, I find the root cause instead of patching the symptom.

Get a QuoteSee my work

Production problems rarely look like the tutorial version. I trace exceptions through logs, reproduce the failing case, check the database and server configuration, and fix the underlying cause with a test where it makes sense so it stays fixed.

Day to day at Technyx that means Linux servers with Apache, Nginx, PHP-FPM, Caddy and cPanel, plus slow queries, race conditions in wallet and time-slot logic, and payment flows that don't reconcile.

What you get

  • Exception tracing and log analysis
  • Slow query diagnosis and index fixes
  • Race conditions and concurrency bugs
  • Nginx, Apache, PHP-FPM and cPanel configuration
  • Deployment problems and migrations gone wrong
  • Ongoing maintenance and small improvements
