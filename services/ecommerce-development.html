Shopify & WooCommerce E-commerce Developer

I build and customize online stores on Shopify, WooCommerce or fully custom stacks, with checkout and inventory logic that stays correct.

Get a QuoteSee my work

Different stores need different tools. For many businesses a customized Shopify or WooCommerce store is the right answer; for others a custom build gives control over pricing, inventory and workflows. I've done both: Shopify theme and cart work on a live store, and my open-source my-store platform, a full Next.js and PostgreSQL storefront and admin.

Whatever the platform, I care about the same things: stock that can't oversell, server-side price and coupon validation, a clear order lifecycle and pages search engines can read.

What you get

  • Shopify Liquid templates, sections and theme customization
  • Cart drawers and bundle selectors on the AJAX Cart API
  • WooCommerce setups
  • Custom stores: catalog, variants, inventory, orders
  • Coupons, order lifecycle and transactional emails
  • Product SEO and structured data

Frequently asked questions

Shopify, WooCommerce or custom: which should I pick?

It depends on budget, catalog complexity and how unusual your workflows are. I'll give you an honest recommendation; often a customized Shopify or WooCommerce store is the faster, cheaper route.

Can you add features to my existing Shopify store?

Yes: custom sections, bundle selectors, cart drawers and performance fixes, built on Shopify's own APIs rather than extra apps where possible.

Do you handle SEO for products?

I add canonical URLs, metadata and structured data such as Product and BreadcrumbList so product pages are eligible for rich results.

Can you integrate a payment gateway into my store?

Yes. See the payment gateway integration service for how I verify payments and handle webhooks safely.
