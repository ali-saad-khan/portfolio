Frequently asked questions

Is Launchpad a real product?

No. It is a portfolio demo by Saad Ali Khan. Pricing, features and data are fictional, and nothing is sold or stored on a server.

Where does the dashboard data come from?

It is mock data bundled with the app. Releases you create are saved in your browser's localStorage only and can be reset from Settings.

Can I use Markdown in release notes?

Yes: headings (#, ##, ###), bold, italic, bullet lists and http(s) links. Raw HTML is escaped, so it is shown as text instead of being executed.

How does annual billing work?

In this demo, annual billing is priced at 20% below twelve months of monthly billing, calculated in a tested pricing module.

Does it respect my theme and motion settings?

Yes. It follows your system light or dark preference unless you pick one, and animations are disabled if you prefer reduced motion.
